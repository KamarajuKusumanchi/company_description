Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical, Inc. is a biopharmaceutical company, which engages
in the identification, acquisition, development, and commercialization of novel
products for the treatment of genetic diseases. Its products include Crysvita,
Mepsevii, Dojolvi, and Evkeeza. The company was founded by Emil D. Kakkis in
April 2010 and is headquartered in Novato, CA.
